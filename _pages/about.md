I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London.

My research lies at the intersection of information theory and machine learning. I am broadly interested in the mathematical foundations of learning and inference, with a focus on generative modeling and statistical learning theory. My work explores how information-theoretic ideas can help us understand learning systems and develop principled methods. I am also interested in recent advances in machine intelligence and their practical applications.
